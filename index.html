chapter 01 · from my heart

A letter for you

There's something I want you to read whenever you're ready.

💌

My Niyuuu,

I know things have been a little difficult lately. Maybe life, the daily routine, family stuff, or even things between us have felt heavy. I know this time is tough for you, and it hasn't been easy for me either. I don't want this letter to make you feel pressured. I just want to remind you of us, of our little memories, and of how much you mean to me. So, chalo… let's go back to our favourite moments. ♡

1 January 2026 — our first call 📞
Do you remember our first call, when you were going to ISKCON? It was the beginning of so many little conversations and memories. Maybe it felt like an ordinary call then, but today it is one of those dates I never want to forget.

19 January 2026 — the first time we met 🥹
Our first meeting! You came to your college gate, I saw you, and you saw me for the first time. Awww, that feeling! We met there and went on my Activa. First, we thought of going to the temple, but it was closed, so we decided to go to ISKCON. We sat near the parking area and talked so much — with a few little silences in between too. Then we went to the laari and had badam shake. After that, we went to ISKCON, had darshan, and I dropped you back. That day was full of simple moments, but honestly, maja aagaya tha. ❤️

1 February 2026 — the hand-holding day 🙈
We went shopping at Metro, then to Alfa and Palladium. And that day… you held my hand for the first time. AWWW! I still remember it. We were both a little scared that someone might see us, but we were having our own little world of fun. We ate there, did absolutely no shopping, and came back. Shopping without shopping — only we could do that! 😂 But the hand-holding? That memory is priceless.

3 February 2026 — one of my most special days 💗
Maybe we had a little argument before meeting that day, but we still met. We sat together, spent time with each other, and made a memory I honestly cannot forget. This day is so special to me because it reminds me how lucky I feel to have met someone like you. Some days become favourites without needing any big plan. This one became one of mine.

You know, I notice little things. Maybe you don't even realise how much I remember — what you like, how your mood changes, when you feel tired of the same daily routine, when you want someone to love you, when you just need someone to understand you, when you want to laugh, and when you simply want to have fun. I may not understand everything perfectly every time, but I do try to notice and understand you.

I also know that sometimes you get tired of things at home, sometimes of the same routine, and sometimes maybe even of me. It's okay to have difficult days. You don't have to hide them from me or pretend to be happy. I just hope you never feel that you have to handle everything alone.

And about me… you know me, but let me tell you again. I'm an overthinker, a little possessive, and sometimes I know I don't handle things perfectly. I don't always like the version of myself I see, so I'm trying to improve. I'm going to the gym, trying to become stronger, taking care of myself, and slowly trying to become the boy who grows into a man — someone more patient, mature, understanding, and better through his actions. I don't want to promise perfection; I want to keep working on myself.

But one thing I want you to know: you're my perfect person not just because I love you, but because you're Niyuuu. Your little ways, your smile, your moods, your silly moments — all the things that make you you. And I'm genuinely proud of you, even for the things you think nobody notices.

Ek dil se baat kahu?
I've never wanted to ask much from you. Bas aaj dil se itna kehna hai: please, aavi rite sad na reso. I wish I could take away every heavy thought, but I know I can't fix everything with a message. I can only remind you that you deserve happiness, peace, care, and lots of reasons to smile. And please remember to be kind to yourself too.

If you're happy, it makes me happy. If you're having a hard day, I hope this little universe can make it a tiny bit lighter. No pressure to reply, no need to pretend — just take this as a big hug wrapped in words.

Now enough emotional talks… 😭😂
Chalo, Niyuuu, welcome to your little universe. Let's eat KitKat, meet teddy, chase some hearts, and do lots of funnn!

With lots of love,
Your person ♡
